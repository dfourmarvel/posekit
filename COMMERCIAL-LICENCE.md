# Commercial licence

posekit is released under the **GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later**. That is a deliberate
choice, and for most people it costs nothing:

- **Using it yourself** — in a clinic, a gym, a classroom, a research project,
  or any internal tool you do not distribute — is free, with no obligation.
- **Building something open** on top of it is free, provided your work is
  released under the AGPL too.

The AGPL asks one thing in return: if you distribute software built on posekit,
or run a modified version as a network service, your changes go back to
everyone. Improvements to the rig should reach the people it was built for.

## When you need this licence instead

If you want to ship a product built on posekit **without** releasing your own
source under the AGPL — a closed-source app, a commercial platform, a white-label
product — you need a separate commercial licence.

Daniel Deladzikunu is the sole copyright holder and can grant one.

**Contact:** danieldeladzikunu@gmail.com

Say what you are building and roughly at what scale, and you will get a
straight answer. Non-profits, student projects and clinics in
resource-constrained settings should ask anyway — the terms are not the same
for everyone, and the point of the project is that people get helped.

## Why dual licensing

This is the same arrangement Qt, Ghostscript and MongoDB use. The open licence
keeps the work available and keeps improvements flowing back; the commercial
exception means a company that wants to keep its own code private pays for that
privilege rather than taking it for free. Nobody loses access, and the ethic
survives contact with commerce.
