Why this is not a nicer sprite sheet

A physiotherapist prescribing a squat after a knee injury does not want a squat — they want a squat to sixty percent, and next week to seventy. A pre-rendered library cannot express that at any file size. This one is a number.

The whole core is about 22 KB gzipped, against 14 MB for a comparable dotLottie set. That gap decides whether an exercise library loads at all on a slow connection.
